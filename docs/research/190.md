# SDD adherence with optimization off and on

## Question

Does token optimization affect Spec-Driven Development adherence for Codex, Claude and Antigravity on an identical spec-first task? [Task #190](https://github.com/taskqorg/agentq/issues/190) supplies the owner request and acceptance A1–A4. This report does not change optimizer or runtime policy.

Decision rule: compare the six SDD clauses within each native off/on pair, retaining actual token counters and duration. Attribute a difference to optimization only with a completed scenario, matched observed model, effective off/on and session-correlated compression evidence. One pair remains descriptive; it cannot establish a general quality effect. Budget: one native six-cell matrix at the runner's existing 120-second turn deadline, plus offline evaluator checks. No login, installation, client configuration or service changes.

## Verdict

**Inconclusive.** Six native attempts are recorded, but zero completed the SDD scenario and none has session-correlated compression evidence. A1 is implemented. A2 has a six-cell attempt record, not six completed native evaluations; A3 cannot establish either degradation or its absence. Native acceptance remains incomplete.

## Recommendation

Retain the scenario and evidence, leave runtime/optimizer policy unchanged, and repeat the identical matrix only after the native prerequisites are verified. Do not interpret equal Codex completion scores, synthetic passes or configured `on` as evidence that optimization preserves SDD adherence.
